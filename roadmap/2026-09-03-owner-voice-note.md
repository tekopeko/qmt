## 1. Landing page content

- Short description under the headline/slogan ("Kvaliteta pokreta prije svega").
- Below it, the offer as sections: **grupni trening, individualni trening,
  rehabilitacija, online trening, prehrana** — for each a "how it looks" text
  (kako izgleda grupni / individualni / poluindividualni trening…).
- Division of labour: **he writes all the text** (he already runs a small blog
  on Shopify and can reuse content from there); we do the app side. Videos he
  records himself.

## 2. Karton polaznika (per-client file)

Each client gets a "karton" under their own account, with:
- **Training log** — what they did last session, weights/kilaža, notes on how
  the training felt. Client can write into it themselves.
- **Nutrition section** — tied to prehrana (see 4).

## 3. Online trening — questionnaire onboarding

Revive the earlier questionnaire idea ("ono što sam već radio, što si mi ti
pomagao"):
- An online **upitnik**; the score automatically places the person into
  **početnici / srednji / napredni**.
- Within their level they pick a goal (e.g. gornji dio tijela, donji dio
  tijela…) and get routed to the matching programmes.

## 4. Prehrana — mini-kuharica + mojimakrosi

- A small cookbook: **30–50 simple healthy recipes** — roughly 10 doručaka,
  10 ručaka, 10 večera. Explicitly "nije nužno za početak" (not launch-blocking).
- Should plug into the **mojimakrosi** app so people can use it there.

## 5. Video hosting (open question)

- For a start: **private/unlisted YouTube** — only option he found with
  unlimited uploads for free.
- Check **Vimeo** (or similar) — what unlimited private hosting costs.

## 6. Payments & membership (open question)

- How payment works end-to-end: when someone pays, **access opens**; when the
  membership lapses, **access closes**.
- Due dates ("dospijeće") and next membership payment visible; **email
  reminders** for both trainings and payments.

## Fit with the existing roadmap (CLAUDE.md)

Items 2 (karton) and 3 (upitnik + programmes) extend roadmap step 3 (workouts /
programmes per client). Item 4 is the concrete shape of step 4 (mojimakrosi
link-up). Items 1, 5, 6 are new: landing copy is content work, video hosting and
payments need research before they become build items.
